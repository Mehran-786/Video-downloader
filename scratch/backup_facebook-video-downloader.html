Step 2: Paste the link into our downloader

Paste the copied URL into the search box above.

  • Automatically un-shortens mobile share links instantly.

Step 3: Click the 'Download Video' button

Our engine queries the direct Facebook CDN stream to fetch all available resolutions.

  • Instant preview and download options appear on screen.

Step 4: Choose your format (HD MP4 or HQ MP3)

Click 'Video (HD)' for high-definition 1080p MP4 or 'Audio (HQ MP3)' to extract only the audio.
