How to Copy an Instagram Video Link

  • Instagram mobile app (iPhone or Android): Tap the Share icon (paper airplane) below the Reel or post, then tap Copy Link. For Stories, tap the three dots in the corner and tap Copy Link.
  • Instagram desktop browser: Click the three-dot menu icon on any post and choose Copy link, or copy the full URL directly from your browser address bar.
  • WhatsApp or Messaging apps: If someone shared an Instagram link with you, long-press the message and copy the URL.
