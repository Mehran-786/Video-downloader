How to Copy a Facebook Video Link

  • Facebook app (iPhone or Android): tap Share under the video or Reel, then Copy Link.
  • Facebook on a computer: click the three-dot menu on the post and choose Copy link, or right-click the post's timestamp and copy the link address.
  • Messenger or WhatsApp: if someone sent you a Facebook video, copy the link from the message itself.

How to Download Facebook Videos on iPhone, Android and Computer

On iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, paste the link and tap Download Video. The file saves to Files, then Downloads. Open it, tap Share and choose Save Video to move it to Photos.

On Android: open this page in Chrome, paste the link and tap Video (HD). The MP4 lands in your Downloads folder and usually appears in Gallery.

On Windows or Mac: paste the link, click Download Video, then click the format you want. The file goes to your browser's Downloads folder.

Can You Download Private Facebook Videos?

No. This tool only downloads videos that anyone can open without logging in. Friends-only posts, private groups and videos with restricted audiences cannot be fetched, and we never ask for your Facebook password to try. Facebook's own Save video button only bookmarks a video inside Facebook; it does not give you a file. If you need a private video, ask the person who posted it to send you the original file. To back up your own videos, Facebook's Download Your Information tool exports them from your account.

Facebook Video Quality: What You Actually Get

Facebook stores each video in several versions. Video (HD) is the highest one available for that clip and Video (Normal) is a smaller version, useful on slow data or when storage is tight. The original upload sets the ceiling: a video uploaded at 480p cannot be turned into 1080p, so the downloader never claims to upscale.

Convert Facebook Video to MP3
