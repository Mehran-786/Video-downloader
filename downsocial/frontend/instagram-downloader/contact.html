Before Contacting: Quick Troubleshooting

  • Is the Instagram account private? If an account is set to private, our tool cannot access it. Test the link in a private/incognito browser window. If it asks you to log in to see the video, it cannot be downloaded.
  • Has the Story expired? Instagram Stories disappear 24 hours after posting. Once expired, they can no longer be retrieved.
  • Copy link from post: Always copy the link directly from the Reel or post's Share icon, not from the general account profile URL.
