How It Works — Universal Social Media Download Guide

A comprehensive walkthrough detailing how to download videos, reels, stories, shorts, and music from any major social media platform using downsocial on iOS, Android, and PC.

Universal 3-Step Process

1

Copy the Share Link

In any app (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, or Threads), tap the Share button and choose Copy Link.

2

Paste into downsocial

Open downsocial in any browser, paste the link into the search bar, and tap Download Video.

3

Choose Format & Save

Pick between Video (HD), Video (Normal), or Audio (HQ MP3). The file saves instantly to your device storage.

Platform-Specific Instructions

1. How to Download from Facebook

Open Facebook on web or mobile. On any public video, Reel, or Story, click the Share icon and select Copy link. Paste into downsocial, and choose between 1080p HD MP4 or HQ MP3.

2. How to Download from Instagram

Navigate to the Instagram Reel, Story, or Carousel post. Tap the three dots (•••) or the paper-airplane share button and select Copy Link. Paste the link into downsocial to download clean media without watermark.

3. How to Download from TikTok (No Watermark)

Open TikTok, tap the Share arrow on the right side of the screen, and select Copy Link. When pasted into downsocial, our engine automatically strips the bouncing watermark logo and provides the clean 60FPS video.

4. How to Download from YouTube

On YouTube or YouTube Shorts, click Share > Copy Link. Paste the link into downsocial to choose your desired quality up to 4K Ultra HD or extract pure 320kbps MP3 audio.

5. How to Download from Snapchat

On any Spotlight clip or public Story, tap the Share button and choose Copy Link. Save vertical 9:16 videos before the 24-hour expiration window closes.

6. How to Download from Meta Threads

On Threads, tap the paper airplane share icon on the post and tap Copy link. downsocial extracts the high-bitrate video or multi-photo album immediately.

Saving Videos on Mobile Devices

📱 For iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari):

  1. Open Safari and visit downsocial.net.
  2. Paste your video link and click Download.
  3. Tap the preferred quality button (e.g. Video HD). Safari will ask "Do you want to download this file?". Tap Download.
  4. Tap the blue Download arrow in the Safari address bar, open the downloaded video, tap Share, and tap Save Video to move it into your Photos app.

🤖 For Android Phones (Chrome / Firefox / Samsung Internet):

  1. Open Chrome and visit downsocial.net.
  2. Paste your link and tap Download.
  3. Click your preferred quality button. The MP4 video will download directly into your Downloads folder and appear automatically in your Gallery.
