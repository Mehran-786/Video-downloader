Privacy Policy — Universal Downloader

At downsocial, protecting your privacy is our foundational principle. This policy explains our zero-knowledge data architecture specifically tailored to our Universal media downloader.

1. Independent Utility Disclaimer

downsocial operates as an independent web application. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or formally associated with Universal or its parent entity. All product and company names are trademarks™ or registered® trademarks of their respective holders.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

You are never required to register an account, sign in, or provide personal credentials (such as your Universal username, email, or password) to use downsocial. We do not track who you are, what profiles you visit, or what videos you download.

3. Ephemeral URL Stream Processing

When you submit a Universal link, our backend processes the URL transiently in temporary memory to resolve the public media stream directly from the platform CDN. Once the direct media download stream is initiated to your browser, the URL request data is discarded.

4. No File Hosting or Archival

We do not host, store, or archive copies of downloaded Universal media files on our servers. Media transfers directly between public CDN servers and your personal device via secure 256-bit SSL encryption.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser LocalStorage strictly for functional client-side preferences (such as your chosen Dark/Light theme mode and language preference) and your recent link history stored locally on your device only. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.
