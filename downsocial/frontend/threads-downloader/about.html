Meta Threads Media Utility

About Threads Video Downloader

Fast, private, and watermark-free tool for downloading public Meta Threads videos, voice notes, and carousel albums.

Our Mission

downsocial Threads Video Downloader was built to address a major frustration: downloading videos, speech clips, and photos from Meta Threads without invasive ads, suspicious redirects, or mandatory logins. We deliver clean, direct downloads straight from public Meta CDN endpoints.

What We Can and Cannot Download

Supported Content

  • Public Threads video posts (up to 1080p Full HD)
  • Threads voice notes & speech clips (MP3 audio)
  • Multi-slide photo & image carousels
  • Quote-thread videos & discussion media
  • Mobile share links (threads.net/t/...)

Not Supported

  • Private Threads profiles requiring follow approvals
  • Deleted or expired Threads posts
  • Direct messages or private conversation threads
  • Account hacking or password retrieval

Technical Architecture & Privacy

When you submit a Threads URL, our backend securely contacts public Meta CDN servers to retrieve direct video streaming links. We operate with a strict zero-log policy: no user identifiers, IP logs, or cookies are stored. Files are transferred directly to your browser without being permanently archived on our servers.
